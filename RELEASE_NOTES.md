SlskME 1.0: a full Soulseek client for J2ME phones, tested on the Nokia C6-00.

**New since Beta 0.4.2**
- **Chat and messages open at the newest message** and stay there as new ones arrive. Up/Down scroll, dragging works on touch screens
- **Coloured nicknames**: your own name, people online and people offline each get their own colour (only the name, not the message)
- **Chat look** (Colors in any chat): follows your phone theme by default, dark themes included, or switch to the phone's native screen. Set a background colour or an image (fill, stretch or tile, with adjustable dimming)
- **Buddies** in the main menu: who's online, alerts when they come and go, they skip your upload queue, search all their files at once
- **Ignore** hides someone's chat and messages. **Ban** stops them browsing or downloading from you. Both in the user menu, undo under Buddies > Blocked
- **Interests**: likes and dislikes, recommendations (yours and global), similar users, who likes what. Pick a thing to search for it
- **Private rooms**: create one, invite and remove members, see the private rooms you're in, leave for good
- **Search history** (Search > History), **room search** (inside a chat room) and **more filters** (Results > Filter > More): minimum bitrate, minimum size, words to hide
- **Whole-folder downloads** get their own subfolder. Unfinished downloads are **.part** files until they're done, and nothing overwrites an existing file
- Failed downloads **retry by themselves**, further apart each time. Retry by hand restarts the count
- **My downloads > Play** opens a finished file in the phone's music player
- **Speed limits** for uploads and downloads, plus a cap on how many files one person can queue
- The share scan is **saved between logins**, so starting up doesn't rescan your whole music folder
- Filenames from old clients that aren't UTF-8 now **show properly and download**

**Install:** copy `SlskME.jar` to the phone and open it (or use the `.jad` for OTA). Install over the previous version to keep your settings, downloads list and wishlist (uninstalling first wipes them).
