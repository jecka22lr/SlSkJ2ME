A fix release for the chat look settings.

**Fixed in 1.1**
- **Previews show up.** Chat look and the colour picker had no preview on the C6-00 (the phone hides image items in forms). Both now draw their own: a mini chat at the top of Chat look that shows your style, name colours, background, dimming and message boxes as you change them, and a sample in the colour picker that follows the sliders

**License:** GPL-3.0-or-later.

**Install:** copy `SlskME.jar` to the phone and open it (or use the `.jad` for OTA). Install over 1.0 to keep your settings, downloads, buddies and chat look.
